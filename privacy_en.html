Privacy Policy for Nolumo

Last updated: 2026-06-14

English · Polski · Deutsch · Español

Overview

Nolumo is a children's educational app designed for ages 3–7. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children.

Data We Do Not Collect

Nolumo does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information about you or your child. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any contact information.
  • We do not track usage data or analytics.
  • We do not use advertising networks or third-party tracking.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not access location, contacts, the camera, or your photos.

The app does request the microphone for two optional features, described below, and recordings stay on your device. The app makes one network call — a one-time in-app purchase processed by your app store (see "In-App Purchases") — which carries no personal data. Apart from that purchase, no data about you or your child leaves the device.

Microphone Access

The app requests access to the device microphone for two optional, on-device-only features:

  • Parent-voice clips — a parent can record short voice clips that the mascot plays back to the child inside the app (for example, words of praise).
  • The Voice studio ("funny voices") — the child can record their own voice and immediately hear it played back transformed for fun (for example a robot or echo voice).

For both features, the recordings:

  • Are processed entirely on the device — they are never uploaded to any server and are not transmitted off the device.
  • Parent-voice clips are stored locally and can be deleted in the app at any time (re-record, clear an individual clip, or erase all of them from the parent area). The Voice-studio recording is temporary — it is played back and then deleted right away (not kept); a new recording would only overwrite it.
  • Are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed with children's privacy in mind. It avoids collecting personal data and is intended to support children's privacy requirements such as the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's data-protection principles under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone.

Local Storage

The app saves game progress (stars, level, achievements, the daily challenge, equipped avatar items) on the device using local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves the device.
  • Is not linked to any personal identity.
  • Can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

No Ads

This app contains no advertising of any kind.

In-App Purchases

The app offers a single optional one-time "Premium" unlock, purchased through your device's app store (Apple App Store / Google Play) behind a parent gate. The store processes the payment in full — the app never receives or stores your payment-instrument or financial information, and the purchase collects no personal data. There are no subscriptions and no consumable ("pay-to-win") purchases.
